Bourbon Dojo

Your personal bourbon collection tracker.

Bourbon Dojo is a mobile app for bourbon enthusiasts. Track every bottle in your collection, log detailed tasting notes and ratings, manage your wishlist, and share sale alerts with friends in private groups.

Features

  • Build and browse your bourbon collection
  • Log tasting notes, ratings, and reviews
  • Maintain a wishlist of bottles to hunt
  • Create or join groups to share sale alerts
  • Sign in securely with Google or Apple
